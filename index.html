Dependency compression · necessary-state mapping

Do not map the usual process. Map what must actually become true.

Dependency Field separates required states, possible pathways, and assumptions treated as requirements. “Get a job” becomes “be hired into acceptable paid work.” “Apply for a job” may be a pathway, but it is not automatically a true dependency.

Local-first prototype. Your mapping data stays in this browser unless you export it. The application is designed to work without transmitting your field data.

Method

Outcome → target state → required states → pathways → assumptions

Dependency Field does not begin by accepting the standard checklist. It asks a narrower question: what must become true for the target state to be true? Some familiar activities survive that test. Others remain useful pathways. Others collapse into assumptions and can be stripped out or treated differently.
